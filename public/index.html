Mechanism of beta blockers? 🤔
Beta blockers competitively antagonize catecholamines at β-adrenergic receptors. They reduce heart rate, contractility & BP by blocking β₁ receptors in the heart.

Key exam point: Propranolol = non-selective (β₁ + β₂), Atenolol = cardioselective (β₁ only) ✅
